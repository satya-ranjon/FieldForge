## 1. Container Pattern Architecture

To eliminate unnecessary page-load latency and preserve operational context, FieldForge avoids creating full-page views whenever a lighter, focused container pattern is superior:

- **Full Page (`PAGE`)**: Used for top-level operational workspaces (Dispatch Board, Radar Canvas, Billing Ledger).
- **Slide-Over Drawer (`DRAWER`)**: Anchored to the right on desktop (480–720px wide). Preserves background board context, scroll position, and filters.
- **Bottom Sheet (`SHEET`)**: Half-height or expandable container on mobile for thumb-friendly quick actions (bidding, quick preview).
- **Focused Modal (`MODAL`)**: High-stakes confirmations, cryptographic signatures, or irreversible actions requiring full user focus.
- **Dedicated Screen Takeover (`TAKEOVER`)**: Full-screen modal on mobile during active job transit or on-site execution.
