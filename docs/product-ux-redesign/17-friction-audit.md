# 17 — Comprehensive Friction & Cognitive Load Audit

> **FieldForge Real-Time Enterprise Field Service Marketplace**  
> Operational Friction Review, Anti-Pattern Deconstruction & Radical Simplifications

---

## 1. Executive Friction Audit Summary

Traditional field service software (ServiceChannel, Field Nation, WorkMarket) and legacy scaffolding suffer from severe ergonomic bloat: multi-step registration walls, confusing status jargon, endless page reloads, and clunky manual photo attachments.

FieldForge applies an aggressive **Friction Elimination Protocol**: every field, click, and container is scrutinized. If a step does not directly contribute to safety, legal compliance, or time-to-value, it is eliminated or deferred.
